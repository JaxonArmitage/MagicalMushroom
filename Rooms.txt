Tent
You find yourself in a small tent. You recognize the tent but don't exactly remember getting it out to go camping. 
There is a sleeping bag and a lantern on the ground. A large tear reveals a very green area to your south.

MossLands
You are in a field but the ground is all... moss? It feels very nice on your feet, not sure why you aren't wearing shoes but you do you. 
The moss seems to have no end but as you move around things get much closer. Your tent stands to your North, There seems to be thick fog 
to your West, You can see very small huts in the distance to your south, and you can hear some splashing to your East!

Pond
You are standing on the dock of a small pond. You seem to have teleported to this dock once you got close enough to the water sound. 
You see fish swimming around, a tackle box,and a large wooden chair. Looks like a cozy fishing spot. You can go back to the Moss to your West.

huts were massive! all the buildings and supplies are abandoned and useless but you see a tree house on the tree next to you! 
A ladder leads up to the tree house. Hopefully you aren't scared of heights because the ladder is not short!
